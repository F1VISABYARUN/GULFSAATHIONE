# Gulf Saathi can encode the law, not the harm

India's recruiting rules, the GCC missions' helplines and the core Gulf labour rules are well enough documented that Gulf Saathi can turn most of them into checks it applies the same way every time. Data on harm to Telangana workers is thin and mostly comes through news reports. The firmest fact is that **a registered recruiting agent (RA) may charge a worker at most Rs 30,000 plus GST, by digital payment or bank draft only, against a receipt** (Emigration Rules 1983, Rule 25, as amended in 2017). Visa, medical, air-ticket and pre-departure lodging costs fall on the foreign employer. Registered and illegal agents can be checked against official lists: MEA's Active RA PDF (about 2,447 RAs as on 08-04-2026) and eMigrate's list of **3,505 notified unregistered agents** (Lok Sabha, 31 July 2026). Every GCC mission publishes an emergency line, and four have toll-free numbers (UAE, Riyadh, Jeddah, Oman). The India-side 24x7 line 1800-11-3090 offers Telugu. On the Gulf side, passport confiscation is illegal in five of six states, Qatar has a QAR 1,000 + 500 + 300 minimum wage, Saudi Arabia's huroob ("absent from work") flag now comes with a 60-day window, and **Kuwait brought back employer exit permits in July 2025**. The harm data is weaker. Official figures for Telangana are limited to emigration clearances (45,213 in 2020 to June 2025) and illegal-recruitment complaints (339 in 2023–2025, ranked 4th nationally, with zero prosecution sanctions). Deaths, stranding and the "15 lakh in the Gulf" figure rest on news and estimates. On speech, the evidence is peer-reviewed and stark: **general-purpose models fail on Telugu** (Whisper large-v2 at 99% WER on FLEURS; GPT-4o Transcribe at 69.3% on Telugu phone calls), and even the best Indic models get about one word in five wrong. That justifies an evaluation built around entities rather than WER alone. Finally, there is no gazetteer from the state gazette in hand. The mandal list is built from NIC district websites, which disagree with themselves in several places.

**How to read this report.** Each fact carries a label for how strong its source is. **[OFFICIAL]** means a government text, portal, Parliament answer, or a mission or ministry page, read directly. **[OFFICIAL-via-news]** means a government figure that was read only through a news outlet quoting it. **[SECONDARY]** means a law firm, academic paper or rights group. **[NEWS]** means news reporting only. **[VENDOR]** means a company's claim about its own or a rival's product. **[UNVERIFIED]** means a single weak source, an unresolved conflict, or an item the researchers inferred themselves. The last section lists everything that still needs checking by hand before the voice agent states it to a worker.

## Rs 30,000 plus GST is the legal ceiling, and the lists are checkable

### The fee cap and payment rules are law, not guidance

Rule 25(1) of the Emigration Rules, as substituted in December 2017, says no RA "shall collect from the worker the service charges more than a maximum of thirty thousand rupees" and must "issue a receipt to the worker for the amount collected" **[OFFICIAL text via legal database]** ([LegitQuest](https://www.legitquest.com/act/emigration-amendment-rules-2017/945B)). The cap already covers scouting, advertising, trade tests, certificate attestation, interview travel, the RA's bank-guarantee costs, the employer's cancellation charges and office overheads, so none of these can be billed on top. Rule 25(3) excludes embassy visa fees, the pre-employment medical, the air ticket and pre-departure board and lodging, with the proviso that these "except goods and services tax, shall be borne by the foreign employer." Rule 25(4) says fees may be collected "only through digital payments or bank draft." The undertaking the RA signs (Annexure-I) says outright that "no payments shall be received in cash." Rule 25(5) requires a board in the RA's office showing the maximum charge, the payment mode, and the Pravasi Bharatiya Bima Yojana (PBBY) insurance and pre-departure orientation charges. MEA's December 2023 advisory states the cap as "**Rs. 30,000 + GST (18%)**" and reports unregistered agents overcharging "to the tune of Rs. 2-5 lakhs" **[OFFICIAL]** ([MEA advisory via Embassy Vilnius](https://www.eoivilnius.gov.in/pdf/Advisory%20circular-19-12-23.pdf); re-issued by [Govt of Goa, Aug 2024](https://www.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/ADVISORY-ON-OVERCHARGING.pdf)). The same advisory says PBBY insurance is mandatory: **Rs 10 lakh cover for a premium of Rs 275 (2 years) or Rs 375 (3 years)**. It also says a genuine job requires an employment contract signed by employer, RA and worker, and that a "tourist visa should only be used for the purpose of tourism."

Two cautions follow. First, MEA's own "RAs" page still shows the pre-2017 cap of "45 days' wages, subject to maximum Rs.20,000" ([MEA ras.htm](https://www.mea.gov.in/ras.htm)). Gulf Saathi must not quote that page. Second, the rule says GST "as applicable"; the 18% figure comes from the advisories. The most recent amendment found, G.S.R. 907(E) of 20 Dec 2023, changed only Rule 8 on security deposits ([Gazette copy](https://www.thc.nic.in/Central%20Governmental%20Rules/Emigration%20Rules,%201983..pdf)). No later change to the cap was found. This is a strong negative finding, but it does not come from a complete search of the Gazette. In practice, a total above **Rs 35,400** (Rs 30,000 + 18%), a demand for cash, no receipt, or a separate charge for visa, medical or ticket is a red flag the agent can apply directly. Treating payment to a personal UPI account as a violation is the researchers' inference. The rule allows "digital payments" and says nothing about whose account receives them.

### Registration and blacklists exist, but one of them cannot be read by machine yet

No person may act as an RA without a Registration Certificate from the Protector General of Emigrants (PGE), issued only through eMigrate (Emigration Act s.10). The Lok Sabha answer of 31 July 2026 confirms that registered RAs are listed publicly on eMigrate and that "a total of **3,505** such unregistered agents have been notified" **[OFFICIAL]** ([Lok Sabha AU2190](https://www.sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AU2190_NjP8nJ.pdf)). The count has been rising: 3,094 in October 2024 and 3,281 in February 2025, both from Rajya Sabha answers seen only as search snippets ([RS 28-11-2024](https://rsdebate.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/753702/1/PQ_266_28112024_U417_p245_p245.pdf); [RS 13-02-2025](https://rsdebate.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/758814/1/PQ_267_13022025_U1034_p251_p252.pdf)). One Siasat report dates the 3,505 figure to December 2025 ([Siasat](https://www.siasat.com/telangana-sees-fourth-highest-illegal-migrations-to-foreign-countries-3350844/)), which does not match the Parliament answer's "as on date" of July 2026.

The registered-agent lists are the easiest to put into the product. MEA's Overseas Employment page links a **"District and State wise list of Active RA" as on 08-04-2026** ([PDF](https://www.mea.gov.in/images/pdf/03-Active-RA-List-21-04-2026.pdf)) and a separate State RA list ([PDF](https://www.mea.gov.in/images/pdf/03-State-RA-List-Report-21-04-2026.pdf)) **[OFFICIAL]** ([MEA Overseas Employment](https://www.mea.gov.in/overseas-employment.htm)). The researchers parsed the Active list: about 2,447 rows, with RA ID, name, signatory, state, district, RC number (format "B-0453/MUM/PER/1000+/5/1563/1984"), contacts and status. By their own count, **about 105 rows are in Telangana**, mostly Hyderabad and Ranga Reddy. That count is an approximation, not an official figure. **TOMCOM is itself a registered RA**: RA9160, RC B-0730/TEL/COM/1000+/5/9160/2015, Active, with address at ITI Campus, Mallepally, Hyderabad 500057 and phones 91-7997973358 and 91-40-23342040 **[OFFICIAL]**. The State RA list also shows entries marked "RC Expired", so a lookup must check status, not just whether a name is present. The list of illegal agents is the real gap. eMigrate is a JavaScript single-page app, and the researchers could not confirm the URL or format (searchable page or downloadable PDF) of either the "List of active RA" or the unregistered-agents list.

### Penalties are weak on paper and rarely enforced in Telangana

Emigration Act s.24 punishes unlicensed recruitment, overcharging and cheating with **up to two years in prison and a fine of up to Rs 2,000 (minimum six months and Rs 1,000)**, doubled for repeat offences, and s.25 extends liability to company officers **[SECONDARY: bare-act site with typos]** ([AdvocateKhoj s.24](https://www.advocatekhoj.com/library/bareacts/emigration/24.php)). MEA refers complaints to state police under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita 2023. The same Lok Sabha answer gives Telangana's illegal-recruitment complaints for 2021–2025 as **74, 63, 132, 96 and 111, with zero prosecution sanctions issued** **[OFFICIAL]** ([AU2190](https://www.sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AU2190_NjP8nJ.pdf)). The law that would replace the Act, the **Overseas Mobility (Facilitation and Welfare) Bill, 2025**, was published in draft in October 2025 ([MEA](https://www.mea.gov.in/overseasmobilitybill2025.htm)). Stakeholder recommendations were released in July 2026 ([Policy Edge](https://www.policyedge.in/p/government-releases-stakeholder-recommendations-on-proposed-overseas-mobility-bill)), and no source shows it has been introduced in Parliament. The 1983 Act and Rule 25 therefore remain the law in force.

All six GCC states are Emigration Check Required (ECR) countries. Holders of ECR passports going there for work need emigration clearance through eMigrate; ECR holders travelling for other purposes do not **[OFFICIAL]** ([MEA](https://www.mea.gov.in/emigration-abroad-for-emp.htm)). MEA's web page says "currently 17" ECR countries, while other MEA-linked documents list 18, adding South Sudan. The researchers did not resolve this, but it does not affect the GCC.

## Five Gulf rules a worker must hear, and how sure we are of each

**Passport confiscation** is prohibited without the worker's consent in the UAE (Decree-Law 33/2021), Saudi Arabia, Oman (Royal Decree 53/2023, its first explicit ban), Qatar (Law 21/2015 Art. 8) and Kuwait (Resolution 143/A/2010) ([Legal500](https://www.legal500.com/developments/press-releases/can-an-employer-keep-your-passport-in-the-uae-heres-what-the-law-says/); [decree.om](https://decree.om/2023/rd20230053/); [GLMM](https://gulfmigration.grc.net/law-no-21-of-2015-regulating-the-entry-exit-and-residence-of-expatriates/?print=pdf); [Arab Times](https://www.arabtimesonline.com/news/it-is-illegal-to-keep-employees-passports-in-kuwait)). Only two fines are firm. Saudi Arabia's HRSD penalty table of August 2026 sets **SAR 1,000/2,000/3,000 per worker by establishment size, SAR 2,000 per domestic worker and SAR 3,000 per shepherd or agricultural worker** **[OFFICIAL]** ([HRSD PDF](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/sites/default/files/2026-08/almkhalfat-wal-qwbat-alkhast-bqta--al-malt-almsandt--alastqdam----en-.pdf)). Qatar's is **up to QAR 25,000** per instance ([GCO](https://www.gco.gov.qa/en/government-of-the-state-of-qatars-response-to-amnesty-international-report-new-name-old-system/)). Bahrain is the exception: the US State Department's 2025 trafficking report says Bahraini law "did not explicitly prohibit passport retention" ([TIP 2025](https://www.ecoi.net/de/dokument/2130519.html)), so for Bahrain the agent should call the practice abusive and point to LMRA, not call it illegal. Because Oman and Qatar allow the employer to hold the passport with the worker's written consent, the agent's wording must be "without your consent".

**Wage protection** systems run in all six states. They monitor whether salaries are paid through banks; they do not guarantee payment. The UAE's Resolution 340/2026, in force 1 June 2026, makes salary due on the **1st of the following month**, with sanctions escalating up to referral to prosecution by day 21 **[SECONDARY]** ([Clyde & Co](https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2026/06/uae-introduces-stricter-wage-protection)). Workers complain to MOHRE on **600590000**, by phone or WhatsApp ([u.ae](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/sector-of-employment/employment-in-the-private-sector/payment-of-wages)). In Saudi Arabia, contract disputes go to HRSD amicable settlement and then the labour court; wage withholding carries SAR 300 per worker, or SAR 3,000 for a domestic worker **[OFFICIAL]** ([HRSD PDF](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/sites/default/files/2026-08/almkhalfat-wal-qwbat-alkhast-bqta--al-malt-almsandt--alastqdam----en-.pdf); [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/xx/en/our-insights/gms-flash-alert/2026/flash-alert-2026-256.html)). Oman requires transfer within 3 days of the due date ([KPMG Oman](https://kpmg.com/om/en/insights/2025/01/ministry-of-labour-updates-to-the-wage-protection-system-in-oman.html)). Bahrain made "WPS 2.0" mandatory from January 2026 ([PwC](https://www.pwc.com/m1/en/tax/documents/2026/social-security-alert-gcc-q1-2026.pdf)). No reliable source was found for Kuwait's wage system or how to complain to its Public Authority for Manpower (PAM). **Only Qatar has a minimum wage for migrants**: QAR 1,000 basic plus QAR 500 housing and QAR 300 food where the employer does not provide them, for every nationality and sector including domestic work, in force since March 2021 ([ILO](https://www.ilo.org/resource/news/qatar-adopts-non-discriminatory-minimum-wage)). An offer for Qatar below these figures is unlawful, which gives the agent a rule it can apply automatically.

**Huroob** is the colloquial name for what Saudi Arabia officially calls an "absent from work" or work-interruption report. It has been softened, not abolished. Under 2025 Qiwa rules, a flagged private-sector worker has **60 days** to transfer, leave or re-contract ([DLA Piper](https://knowledge.dlapiper.com/dlapiperknowledge/globalemploymentlatestdevelopments/2025/saudi-arabia-grants-expat-workers-60-days-to-avoid-being-classified-as-absent)). A Qiwa clarification reported in September–October 2026 adds that a worker with **more than 12 months in the Kingdom** can transfer despite the absent status, with the new employer paying outstanding fees ([KPMG 2026-256](https://kpmg.com/xx/en/our-insights/gms-flash-alert/2026/flash-alert-2026-256.html)). Both rules come only from law-firm summaries **[SECONDARY]**. The 2021 Labour Reform Initiative gave private-sector workers job mobility after one year and the right to obtain exit/re-entry and final-exit visas without the employer's permission. It **excluded domestic workers**, who follow Musaned rules and had an amnesty that appears to have lapsed around May 2026 ([Fragomen](https://www.fragomen.com/insights/additional-details-released-on-labor-reform-initiative-for-the-private-sector.html); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/saudi-arabia-extends-grace-period-for-runaway-domestic-workers-1.500342505)). Gulf Saathi should never tell a user that huroob "no longer exists". It should tell them to check their status on Absher, Qiwa or Musaned at once.

**Working on a visit visa** is illegal in all six states. The worker bears the fine, detention, deportation and any re-entry ban, not just the employer. Saudi Arabia's official table fines visa misuse in domestic work SAR 10,000 per worker plus a one-year recruitment ban for the employer ([HRSD](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/sites/default/files/2026-08/almkhalfat-wal-qwbat-alkhast-bqta--al-malt-almsandt--alastqdam----en-.pdf)). The UAE's reported minimum of AED 10,000 for the worker is **[UNVERIFIED]** ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/living-in-uae/visa-immigration/dh5-million-fine-7-uae-visa-violations-that-could-lead-to-jail-and-deportation-1.500376438)). Kuwait's overstay fines under Decree-Law 114/2024 conflict between sources. Since Telangana fraud cases repeatedly follow the pattern "go on a visit visa, the permit comes later", this is the agent's most important single warning.

**Exit permits and kafala** have moved in opposite directions. Qatar abolished exit permits (2018 for workers under the Labour Law; Decision 95/2019 for everyone else) and the employer's no-objection requirement for changing jobs (Law 18/2020) ([GCO](https://www.gco.gov.qa/en/focus/labour-reform/); [ILO](https://www.ilo.org/resource/overview-qatars-labour-reforms)). **Kuwait went backwards**: Ministerial Circular 2/2025, effective 1 July 2025, requires private-sector expatriates to get an employer-approved exit permit through the Sahel app, valid for 7 days, for any departure ([EY](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/technical/tax-alerts/new-exit-permit-rule-effective-1-july-2025); [HRW](https://www.hrw.org/news/2025/06/15/kuwaits-exit-permit-requirement-puts-migrant-workers-at-risk)). Governments in Saudi Arabia, Qatar and Oman describe kafala as reformed. UN experts in April 2026 still urged Saudi Arabia to "effectively abolish" it ([HRW, May 2026](https://www.hrw.org/news/2026/05/19/un-experts-sound-alarm-over-saudi-arabias-abusive-labor-governance-system)). The fair summary is that employer-tied residency and weaker protection for domestic workers persist everywhere.

## Telangana's harm record is mostly news, with three official anchors

Three official figures can be cited. The first is emigration clearances: a Lok Sabha reply of 8 Aug 2025 gives **45,213 Telangana clearances for 2020 to June 2025**, peaking at 11,990 in 2022, compared with Andhra Pradesh's 71,317 **[OFFICIAL-via-news]** ([The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/telangana/45000-people-migrated-out-of-telangana-for-blue-collar-jobs-from-2020-to-2025)). The second is the illegal-recruitment complaint series above (Telangana fourth nationally, 339 complaints in three years, 99 action-taken reports) ([Siasat, Aug 2026](https://www.siasat.com/andhra-pradesh-telangana-top-overseas-job-scam-complaints-mea-3518389/); [AU2190](https://www.sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AU2190_NjP8nJ.pdf)). The third is national death and grievance data. ## Off-the-shelf speech models break on Telugu calls; Indic models still miss one word in five

The peer-reviewed evidence is consistent. OpenAI's own paper reports **Whisper large-v2 at 99.0% word error rate (WER) on FLEURS Telugu**, compared with 17.5% for Tamil. It names Telugu as a language that underperforms its training data, blaming linguistic distance and the tokenizer ([Whisper paper, Table 13](https://cdn.openai.com/papers/whisper.pdf)). AI4Bharat's IndicVoices test, which uses natural Telugu speech including 8 kHz telephony, measured **Whisper at 151.9%**, Azure at 53.3%, Google USM at 40.3% and IndicASR at 26.8% ([arXiv 2403.01926](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.01926v1)). The closest match to Gulf Saathi's setting is **Voice of India (Interspeech 2026)**, a closed benchmark of unscripted telephone conversations. On Telugu it reports **Sarvam Audio 18.2%, Amazon Transcribe 19.7%, Gemini 3 Pro 21.9%, IndicConformer 23.7%, GPT-4o Transcribe 69.3% and GPT-4o Mini Transcribe 81.2%** ([arXiv 2604.19151](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.19151)). The Telugu column was matched by the researchers from extracted text, and several authors are affiliated with Sarvam, so verify the table against the PDF before putting it on a slide. The same study found that **utterances under 2 seconds** (one-word turns like "avunu" or "ledu") and poor audio roughly double error rates. For example, Amazon rose from 10.45% to 18.74% WER on short utterances. For code-mixed speech, the only public Telugu-English numbers come from CoSTA's 2.3-hour high-code-mixing subset, where systems score 34–44% WER ([arXiv 2406.10993](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.10993)). No large open Telugu-English code-switched corpus, and none recorded over telephone lines, was found. Claims of an 8 kHz penalty specific to Telugu (+6.3 points on the G.711 phone codec) are **[VENDOR]** only ([caller.digital](https://caller.digital/blog/8khz-narrowband-telephony-asr-tts-benchmark-india-2026)). A fine-tuned community Whisper reaches 9.65% on FLEURS Telugu ([HF](https://huggingface.co/vasista22/whisper-telugu-large-v2)), which shows the gap comes from missing data and can be fixed.

How to evaluate matters as much as which model to use. Normalisers in the style of Whisper's are "fundamentally flawed when applied to Indic scripts" and make scores look artificially good ([EMNLP 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.02449)). Voice of India replaces single-reference WER with an **orthography-informed lattice WER**, which accepts valid spelling variants and gives more stable rankings than WER against a single reference transcript. Entity-heavy speech is hardest even in Indian-accented English: Whisper scored 11.2% on entity-heavy prompts against 6.2% on read speech ([Svarah](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2305.15760)). A defensible test harness reports the following:

- **CER**, since Telugu is agglutinative;
- **WER after Indic-aware normalisation**;
- **entity error rate**, computed as 1 − entity recall, where an entity counts only if all its words are right ([arXiv 2604.00610](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.00610));
- **digit-string accuracy** for passport, phone and amount values;
- **negation-flip rate**;
- **task success**, meaning whether the right risk signal was extracted.

These should be measured on a team-recorded Telugu-English test set played over 8 kHz and 16 kHz, with the CC-BY-4.0 IndicVoices Telugu test set (5 h) as an external reference. Sarvam's proposed thresholds, an entity preservation score of at least 0.95 for high-stakes domains ([Sarvam](https://www.sarvam.ai/blogs/evaluating-indian-language-asr)), are a useful target but are **[VENDOR]** guidance.

## Conclusion

Most of what Gulf Saathi needs to tell a worker can be written as fixed rules, each backed by an official source: a fee total above Rs 35,400, payment in cash, no receipt, an RA that is not on MEA's list or has an expired certificate, a visit visa offered for work, a Qatar wage below QAR 1,800 all-in, a passport held without consent, and a Kuwaiti departure that needs the employer's Sahel approval. These are the product's strongest claims, and the voice layer should state them with confidence. Harm statistics, by contrast, belong in the pitch rather than in the agent's answers to workers. Telangana's official record of zero prosecution sanctions across 476 complaints in five years shows that the formal enforcement chain is not protecting workers. That makes a pre-departure check more valuable, because it does not depend on the state taking action after the fact.

The speech evidence changes the engineering priority. Switching to a better ASR model is not enough. Even the best Telugu systems drop entities often enough that every agent name, RC number, amount and city must be read back and confirmed. The test that matters is entity error and task success on short Telugu-English phone turns, which no public benchmark measures. The two largest open risks for the product are the eMigrate illegal-agent list, which cannot yet be ingested reliably, and helpline numbers that change without notice. A verified ingestion job and a quarterly re-check of the helpline numbers would close more risk than any single additional research finding.
