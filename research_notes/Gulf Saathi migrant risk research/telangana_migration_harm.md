# Telangana Gulf Migration and Harms to Workers: Data, Dates, Sources

Research date: 21 Nov 2026. Source-quality labels used below:
- **[OFFICIAL]**: a Parliament reply or government data, read through a primary page or a news report quoting it. Primary sansad.in and mea.gov.in pages returned HTTP 403 to the fetch tool, so most "official" figures here were read through news outlets that quote the reply. They should be checked against sansad.in before publication.
- **[NEWS]**: claims made only by news outlets.
- **[ESTIMATE]**: figures from activists or politicians, not official counts.

## Q1. How many Telangana workers are in the Gulf? (state and North Telangana districts)

### Takeaway
No official count exists of Telangana workers living in the Gulf. The only hard official numbers are emigration clearances (EC) for ECR-passport holders: 45,213 from Telangana in 2020–2025, compared with 25,099 in 2016 alone. The widely quoted "~15 lakh Telangana workers in the Gulf" is an unofficial estimate.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL, via news] Lok Sabha written reply by MoS External Affairs Kirti Vardhan Singh, 8 Aug 2025, to a question from Khammam MP Ramasahayam Raghurama Reddy: **45,213 Telangana workers received emigration clearance over 2020–2025.** By year: 2020: 2,984; 2022: 11,990 (highest); 2024: 9,850; 2025: 5,642 (probably January–June only, because the comparison figures run "2020 to June 2025"). — [The News Minute, 9 Aug 2025](https://www.thenewsminute.com/telangana/45000-people-migrated-out-of-telangana-for-blue-collar-jobs-from-2020-to-2025)
  - The same article gives gender splits that do not reconcile with its totals. For example, 2024 lists 10,151 men, which is more than the 9,850 total. **Use only the totals.**
  - Other states, 2020 to June 2025 (same reply): Tamil Nadu 96,619; Kerala 77,722; Andhra Pradesh 71,317; Karnataka 24,700; Uttar Pradesh ~5.49 lakh; Bihar ~2.84 lakh. — [The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/telangana/45000-people-migrated-out-of-telangana-for-blue-collar-jobs-from-2020-to-2025)
- [NEWS, citing eMigrate data] Telangana ECs: 25,099 in 2016, 13,388 in 2019, 2,984 in 2020, **10,885 in 2025**. The outlet says Telangana had not returned to pre-Covid levels, while Andhra Pradesh had. — [Telugu360](https://www.telugu360.com/ecr-migration-andhra-surpasses-pre-covid-levels-telangana-not-yet/)
  - Conflict: TNM's 5,642 for 2025 is probably a part-year figure, and Telugu360's 10,885 is probably the full calendar year. Neither outlet states its period explicitly.
- [OFFICIAL, via news] In a Lok Sabha reply on eMigrate data (Pravasi Bharatiya Bima Yojana policies by state), Telangana ranks among the top six states, after UP (14,15,861 policies), West Bengal, Tamil Nadu, Rajasthan and Punjab. The same reply says nearly 1 crore Indians live in Gulf nations. — [Kashmir Life](https://kashmirlife.net/nearly-one-crore-indians-reside-in-gulf-nations-govt-tells-lok-sabha-415823/)
- [ACADEMIC, secondary] Telangana and Andhra Pradesh together accounted for more than 751,000 emigration clearances in 2007–2016. — [IJCRT paper, 2024](https://ijcrt.org/papers/IJCRT2407987.pdf)
- [ESTIMATE/NEWS] "Estimates place Telangana's Gulf workforce at around **15 lakh**, although an updated official count is unavailable." The same report says Telangana's Gulf migrant welfare policy is still pending, and that families in Jagtial, Nizamabad and Hyderabad's Old City depend on Gulf earnings (published ~Nov 2026). — [NewsAge](https://www.newsage.in/telangana-gulf-workers-war-returnees/)
- [ESTIMATE/NEWS] Other reports say ~15 lakh Telangana workers are in the Gulf and ~30 lakh have returned. A CM statement says 15 lakh *families* depend on Gulf jobs. The CM also announced a "Telangana Gulf & Overseas Welfare Board" in April 2024. — [Article 14](https://article-14.com/post/millions-of-indians-in-the-gulf-working-in-difficult-degrading-conditions-demand-govt-intervention-safety-net-671864b9af379); [Siasat](https://www.siasat.com/will-setup-special-board-for-gulf-workers-welfare-telangana-cm-3009916/)
- [NEWS] North Telangana Gulf-belt districts are said to feature regularly in an annual Union government list of the top 100 districts sending workers abroad. The report's date is not clear. — [Deccan Chronicle](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/nation/gulf-migrants-pin-hopes-on-welfare-measures-before-ls-polls-885184)
- [CONTEXT] ECs cover only ECR-passport holders (education below Class 10) going to 18 notified countries for work. They count clearances, not people. — [Telangana.com NRI guide](https://telangana.com/nri.html); [Emigration Act, 1983](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emigration_Act,_1983)

### Inferences
- The EC figures for 2020–2025 (~45k) are far below the 15-lakh stock estimate, for three reasons:
  - ECs miss non-ECR (Class 10+) workers and anyone travelling on visit or tourist visas.
  - ECs count flows, not the number of people abroad.
  - The 15-lakh figure is a cumulative political or activist estimate.
- For a pitch, present 15 lakh only as "estimated (unofficial)", and use the official EC series to show a dated, verifiable trend.
- The sharp fall in ECs since 2016 does not mean migration fell by the same amount. Some of it may have moved to visit-visa routes outside eMigrate, which is consistent with the visit-visa scam pattern in Q4. This is an inference, not a proven fact.

### Gaps
- No district-wise official EC data was found for Jagtial, Karimnagar, Rajanna Sircilla, Nizamabad, Kamareddy or Nirmal.
- No Telangana migration survey (CDS/IIMAD-style) was found.
- The primary sansad.in text of the 8 Aug 2025 reply could not be fetched (HTTP 403). The question number is unknown.

## Q2. Deaths of Indian workers in the GCC, by year, and Telangana-specific deaths and ex gratia

### Takeaway
MEA told the Rajya Sabha that 37,740 Indian workers died abroad in 2021–2025, more than 20 a day. 32,608 of these deaths, about 86%, were in the five Gulf countries excluding Bahrain. No official Telangana death count was found. The proxy is ex gratia: about 165–169 families had received Rs 5 lakh each by 2025.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL, via news] Rajya Sabha reply by MoS Kirti Vardhan Singh (reported as 29 Jan 2026), deaths of Indian workers abroad:

  | Year | Deaths |
  |---|---|
  | 2021 | 8,234 |
  | 2022 | 6,614 |
  | 2023 | 7,291 |
  | 2024 | 7,747 |
  | 2025 | 7,854 |
  | **Total** | **37,740** |

  — [India TV, 2 Apr 2026](https://www.indiatvnews.com/news/india/over-20-indian-workers-died-abroad-daily-in-last-five-years-majority-in-gulf-government-data-2026-04-02-1036046); [Northeast News](https://nenews.in/neighbours/over-20-indian-workers-died-daily-abroad-in-last-5-years-says-government-data/46722/)
- [OFFICIAL, via news] Same reply, deaths by country for 2021–2025:
  - UAE: 12,380
  - Saudi Arabia: 11,757
  - Kuwait: 3,890
  - Oman: 2,821
  - Malaysia: 1,915
  - Qatar: 1,760
  - Gulf total excluding Bahrain: 32,608, about 18 a day

  — [Telangana Today](https://telanganatoday.com/over-20-indian-workers-died-abroad-daily-in-five-years-government-data); [Business Standard, Apr 2026](https://www.business-standard.com/immigration/20-indians-died-abroad-daily-in-5-years-uae-saudi-top-fatality-numbers-126040700823_1.html); [p30work.org, 2 Apr 2026](https://p30work.org/2026/04/02/disturbing-statistics-about-the-conditions-of-indian-workers-abroad-reveal-that-/)
- [OFFICIAL, via news] MEA reply, Feb 2024: Indian deaths in Kuwait were 731 in 2022 and 708 in 2023. — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fkuwait-where-over-1400-indian-workers-died-in-past-two-years-3064187)
- [Historical] Earlier counts:
  - 24,570 Gulf deaths in 2012–2018, per a 2018 PTI/CHRI report. — [p30work.org](https://p30work.org/2026/04/02/disturbing-statistics-about-the-conditions-of-indian-workers-abroad-reveal-that-/)
  - Reports of "34,000 deaths in the Gulf since 2014". — [BHRRC](https://www.business-humanrights.org/en/latest-news/india-34000-migrant-workers-have-died-in-the-gulf-since-2014-activists-criticise-lack-of-protections/)
- [ESTIMATE] An activist cited by Caravan estimates "close to 2,000" Telangana workers died in the Gulf over the last decade. This is not an official count. — [The Caravan](https://caravanmagazine.in/labour/migrant-workers-indian-gulf-deaths-natural-causes)
- [NEWS, quoting state officials] Telangana's Rs 5 lakh ex gratia:
  - The scheme covers deaths reported **after 12 Dec 2023** in 7 countries: Bahrain, Kuwait, Iraq, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia and the UAE.
  - By 20 Nov 2025: **165 families paid** and 120 applications pending.
  - Families must apply within 6 months of the death or of receiving the body.
  - Source quoted: Manda Bheem Reddy, vice-chairman of the state NRI advisory committee.

  — [Deccan Chronicle, 20 Nov 2025](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/southern-states/telangana/deaths-of-telangana-migrants-rise-in-gulf-1918437)
  - Another report gives 169 families paid, totalling Rs 8.45 crore in 2024–25. — [Deccan Chronicle](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/southern-states/telangana/tg-releases-rs-5-l-each-to-kin-of-gulf-victims-1864367)
  - Batch releases include Rs 3.3 crore (66 families, which is my own division at Rs 5 lakh each; the article does not give a count) and Rs 1.55 crore for 31 families from Jagtial. — [Siasat](https://www.siasat.com/telangana-govt-transfers-rs-3-3-crore-to-kin-of-gulf-migrant-workers-3200453/); [Siasat](https://www.siasat.com/telangana-govt-sanctions-rs-1-55-crore-ex-gratia-for-families-of-deceased-gulf-workers-3307190/)
  - Officials claim Telangana is the only state paying Rs 5 lakh for deaths abroad. This is unverified.
- [NEWS, case] A family whose Gulf worker died *after returning* to Telangana sought ex gratia; such deaths fall outside the scheme. — [Siasat](https://www.siasat.com/gulf-worker-dies-after-return-to-telangana-family-seeks-rs-5l-ex-gratia-3445212/)
- [NEWS, case] A 34-year-old from Jagtial died in Qatar after a prolonged illness (18 Mar), and his body was repatriated. — [Siasat](https://www.siasat.com/telangana-worker-dies-in-qatar-after-illness-body-repatriated-to-native-village-3440776/)

### Inferences
- About 165 ex gratia payments in roughly 2 years, plus 100–120 applications pending, suggests on the order of 130–150 recorded Telangana Gulf-worker deaths a year. This is a rough inference: the count is limited by people who apply and by the 6-month deadline.
- Many deaths are recorded as "natural causes" (cardiac or respiratory) with no inquiry (Caravan). This is a strong problem-statement angle.

### Gaps
- No official year-wise or district-wise death count for Telangana was found.
- The exact date and question number of the Rajya Sabha reply were not confirmed from a primary page. The country-wise Bahrain figure was not given.

## Q3. Complaints by Indian workers (MADAD, eMigrate, missions), by country and category

### Takeaway
Gulf missions logged 33,252 worker complaints from March 2021 to December 2023. All missions logged 80,985 grievances in 2021–2025, rising from 16,263 in 2024 to 22,479 in 2025. Wage non-payment and passport retention are named categories, but no category counts were found.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] Lok Sabha Q.No. 2105, "Indian Workers in Gulf Countries" (MEA): **33,252 complaints** from Indian workers at Gulf missions, Mar 2021–Dec 2023. Categories named, without counts:
  - delayed or non-payment of salaries
  - retention of passports
  - unfair working conditions
  - substandard accommodation
  - extended working hours
  - ill-treatment or harassment

  — [MEA Lok Sabha Q2105](https://www.mea.gov.in/lok-sabha.htm?dtl/37435/QUESTION+NO2105+INDIAN+WORKERS+IN+GULF+COUNTRIES) (seen via search snippet; the page returned 403)
- [OFFICIAL, via news] Rajya Sabha reply on 2021–2025:
  - Total: **80,985 grievances** across missions.
  - By country: UAE 16,965; Saudi Arabia 12,988; Malaysia 8,333; Maldives 2,981; Myanmar 2,548 (1,863 of them in 2025); Cambodia 2,531; Laos 2,416.
  - By year: 2024: 16,263; 2025: 22,479, a ~38% rise. The outlet's claim that complaints "doubled" is wrong.

  — [p30work.org, 2 Apr 2026](https://p30work.org/2026/04/02/disturbing-statistics-about-the-conditions-of-indian-workers-abroad-reveal-that-/)
- [OFFICIAL, via news] A Nov 2019 reply recorded 15,501 complaints from Gulf workers in Jan–Oct 2019, mostly about:
  - late or non-payment of wages
  - denial of labour rights
  - non-issue of residence permits
  - denial of overtime and weekly off
  - forced long hours

  — [Delhi Post](https://delhipostnews.com/gulf-deaths-parliament-responds-with-madad-portal-rti-activist-urges-for-detailed-study/); [The Quint](https://www.thequint.com/news/india/exploited-in-the-gulf-saudi-and-qatar-the-worst-for-indian-workers)
- [OFFICIAL] Grievance channels (MoS reply on Saudi Arabia): walk-in, email, 24x7 multilingual helplines, WhatsApp, MADAD/CPGRAMS/eMigrate, and social media. — [The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/india/indian-embassy-consulates-working-to-assist-stranded-workers-in-saudi-arabia-govt)

### Inferences
- The rise in complaints from Myanmar, Laos and Cambodia in 2025 shows scam-compound trafficking growing as a new harm category next to Gulf labour abuse. Telangana youth appear in both (see Q4).

### Gaps
- No category-wise counts (wages, passport, agent cheating) or Telangana-origin complaint counts were found for MADAD or eMigrate.

## Q4. Illegal and fake recruiting agents, visit-visa scams, and stranded or trafficked Telangana workers

### Takeaway
MEA data tabled in the Lok Sabha ranks Telangana **4th nationally for illegal-recruitment complaints**: 339 over three years, including 111 in 2025. Police cases in Jagtial and Nizamabad, and trafficking to Myanmar, Laos and the Russian army, are documented in the news.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL, via news] MEA data tabled in the Lok Sabha, fake overseas-recruitment complaints over "the last three years":

  | State | Complaints | Action Taken Reports (ATRs) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Andhra Pradesh | 1,495 | 35 |
  | Kerala | 625 | – |
  | Tamil Nadu | 357 | – |
  | **Telangana** | **339** | **99** |

  — [Siasat, 4 Aug 2026](https://www.siasat.com/andhra-pradesh-telangana-top-overseas-job-scam-complaints-mea-3518389/)
  - For 2025 alone, Telangana had 111 complaints (4th, after AP, Kerala and Delhi). eMigrate listed 3,505 unregistered or illegal agents nationally as of Dec 2025. — [Siasat](https://www.siasat.com/telangana-sees-fourth-highest-illegal-migrations-to-foreign-countries-3350844/); Lok Sabha annexure (state-wise complaints, ATRs, FIRs and prosecutions for 2021–2025): [sansad.in AU2190](https://www.sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AU2190_NjP8nJ.pdf) (could not fetch; 403)
- [NEWS] More than 4,000 police complaints were filed nationally against illegal recruiting agents from 2021 to June 2024, with police acting on only ~10%. — [New Lines Magazine](https://newlinesmag.com/spotlight/a-network-of-agents-in-india-is-driving-job-scams-and-illegal-migration/)
- [NEWS] **Jagtial, 2026, Saudi "shepherd" fraud:**
  - The agent was booked for cheating after sending a 22-year-old to Saudi Arabia for desert shepherd work instead of the promised job.
  - The family paid Rs 90,000 in instalments between Dec 2025 and Jan 2026.
  - The youth's viral rescue videos prompted the case. No arrest was reported.

- [NEWS] **Jagtial, 20 Feb 2026, Laos cyber-fraud trafficking:**
  - Jagtial Rural police (SP Ashok Kumar) arrested 5 people.
  - Victims paid Rs 75,000–1.05 lakh each, travelled on Bangkok tourist visas, and were forced into cyber fraud in the Golden Triangle.
  - The prime accused was promised 13,000 yuan (~Rs 1.75 lakh) per recruit. A lookout circular led to his arrest at Hyderabad airport on 17 Feb 2026.

  — [Telangana Today, 20 Feb 2026](https://telanganatoday.com/jagtial-police-bust-gang-trafficking-youth-to-laos-for-cyber-fraud-five-arrested)
- [NEWS] **Nizamabad, Jan 2023:** Agent "Sheikh Bashir" allegedly took about Rs 2 crore from more than 500 Gulf-job aspirants across several districts. An FIR was filed and a manhunt launched. — [ETV Bharat, 10 Jan 2023](https://www.etvbharat.com/english/state/telangana/job-aspirants-duped-of-rs-2-crore-in-telanganas-nizamabad-accused-on-the-run/na20230110140545589589217)
- [NEWS, pattern; no named cases] **Nizamabad, Kamareddy, Bodhan and Armoor, Oct 2026:**
  - Unregistered agents charge Rs 1.5–3 lakh for "supervisor or store-manager" jobs.
  - They issue fake contracts and send people on tourist or visit visas with promises of permits "after arrival".

  — [The Hans India, 6 Oct 2026](https://www.thehansindia.com/news/cities/nizamabad/fraudulent-recruiters-trap-nizamabad-youth-in-gulf-russia-1129333)
- [NEWS] **Hyderabad (Lalaguda):** 2 people were arrested for supplying fake visas and offer letters to applicants from Hyderabad and Nizamabad. — [Telangana Today](https://telanganatoday.com/two-held-for-job-visa-fraud-in-secunderabads-lalaguda)
- [NEWS] **Myanmar scam hubs, 2025:**
  - The Telangana Cyber Security Bureau arrested 8 people (15 agents identified) for trafficking 24 Telangana residents.

  - Telangana youths were among those freed after the Feb 2025 KK Park raids.

- [NEWS] **Russia, 2024:**
  - Mohammed Afsan (30, Hyderabad Old City) and Mohammed Sufiyan (Narayanpet) were sent to Russia as army "helpers".
  - One of them died in the Russia–Ukraine war.

  — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fone-of-2-telangana-youths-forced-to-work-for-russian-army-dies-fighting-ukraine-war-2924974)
- [NEWS, undated or older] About 6,000 Telangana workers were reported in Gulf jails, an older Gulf News report whose date is not confirmed. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/asia/india/6000-workers-from-telangana-in-gulf-jails-1.1429310)

### Inferences
- Telangana ranks 4th in illegal-recruitment complaints despite much lower EC volume than Kerala or Tamil Nadu. This suggests a high rate of fraud per migrant in Telangana.
- Telangana's 99 ATRs on 339 complaints are a higher action rate than Andhra Pradesh's 35 on 1,495.
- The usual fraud pattern is: a visit or tourist visa, a fee of Rs 75k–3 lakh, a fake contract, and a job that differs on arrival or forced scam work.

### Gaps
- Telangana prosecution and conviction counts (sansad.in annexure; 403).
- No case was found for Rajanna Sircilla or Karimnagar.
- No Iraq-stranding case was found in this search.
- The arrest status of the Saudi shepherd agent is unknown.

## Q5. Remittances and economic dependence

### Takeaway
Telangana received **8.1% of India's inward remittances in 2023-24**, 4th among states (RBI). No Gulf-specific or district-level remittance figure exists. The GCC accounted for 38% of India's total remittances.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] RBI Bulletin, March 2025 (6th remittance survey, 2023-24): **Telangana 8.1%**, ranked 4th after Maharashtra (20.5%), Kerala and Tamil Nadu. The RBI links Telangana's share partly to *students* abroad who stay on to work, not only to Gulf workers. — [RBI Bulletin Mar 2025 (PDF)](https://rbidocs.rbi.org.in/rdocs/Bulletin/PDFs/03AR190320254C8A48EE0679434B89E59CB5D5CDA361.PDF)
- [OFFICIAL, via news] GCC countries accounted for 38% of India's remittances in 2023-24. Advanced economies overtook the Gulf in share. — [Business Standard, Mar 2025](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/economy/news/remittances-to-india-from-advanced-economies-surpass-gulf-inflows-rbi-125031901164_1.html)
- [SECONDARY] Telangana's share is equivalent to roughly Rs 79,321 crore. This is derived by a third party from the RBI's 8.1%. — [INDmoney](https://www.indmoney.com/blog/personal-finance/remittances-to-india-explained)
- [NEWS] Households in Jagtial, Nizamabad and Hyderabad's Old City depend on Gulf earnings. — [NewsAge](https://www.newsage.in/telangana-gulf-workers-war-returnees/)
- [OPINION/ANALYSIS] An analysis piece argues that expatriate workers buoy the economies of five states. — [Scroll](https://scroll.in/article/1091871/expatriate-workers-buoy-economies-of-five-indian-states-politicians-must-stop-ignoring-them)

### Inferences
- Telangana's 8.1% probably includes much non-Gulf money from the Hyderabad IT and student diaspora in the US, UK and other advanced economies. Do not attribute it entirely to Gulf workers.

### Gaps
- No Gulf-only or district-level remittance data was found for North Telangana.
- No household survey of Gulf dependence in these districts was found.
- No Telangana Migration Survey exists, to our knowledge.
