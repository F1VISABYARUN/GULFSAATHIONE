# Official help channels for Indian (Telangana) migrant workers in the GCC, and Telangana government support

All pages below were checked on 2026-11-21. "Official" means the number or email appears on the mission's own site or on mea.gov.in. "Unverified (news)" means it appears only in news reports or third-party sites.

Access notes from this session:
- These sites could be fetched directly: indembassyuae.gov.in, cgidubai.gov.in, eoiriyadh.gov.in, indianembassyqatar.gov.in, indemb-oman.gov.in (the live Oman embassy domain), and mea.gov.in.
- These sites could not be reached from the research environment (502/503 or DNS failure): cgijeddah.gov.in, eoimuscat.gov.in (no DNS; the Oman mission is at indemb-oman.gov.in), indembkwt.gov.in, eoibahrain.gov.in, madad.gov.in, and tomcom.telangana.gov.in.
- emigrate.gov.in loads only as a JavaScript app, so nothing on it could be read.
- For Jeddah, Kuwait and Bahrain, the numbers below come from official secondary pages: the Riyadh embassy site, the Qatar embassy site, and MEA's country and emergency pages. They were not taken from those missions' own sites.

## Q1. Mission helplines in the six GCC countries

### Takeaway
Every GCC mission publishes a 24x7 emergency or labour line. The toll-free lines most useful to a worker in danger are:

| Country | Toll-free line | Run by |
|---|---|---|
| UAE | 800 46342 | PBSK Dubai (covers the whole UAE) |
| Saudi Arabia (Riyadh) | 800 247 1234 | Embassy, Riyadh |
| Saudi Arabia (Jeddah) | 800 244 0003 | Consulate, Jeddah |
| Oman | 80071234 | Embassy, Muscat |

Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain mostly rely on mobile or WhatsApp lines. Kuwait's 24x7 number is printed with a missing digit on MEA's page, so it needs care.

## Q2. India-side channels: MADAD, eMigrate/OWRC helpline, PoE Hyderabad

### Takeaway
- MADAD (madad.gov.in) is MEA's consular grievance portal, launched 21 Feb 2015. It covers salary dues, repatriation, imprisonment, mortal remains, compensation and tracing of missing persons.
- The 24x7 toll-free number **1800-11-3090** is official. MEA labels it both "Madad Helpline" and the OWRC helpline. From abroad, call +91-124-2341002.

### Cited Findings
- MEA launched MADAD on 21 February 2015. — [MEA Consular Complaints & Grievances](https://www.mea.gov.in/consular-complaints-and-grievances.htm)
  - It links all missions and MEA branch secretariats, including Hyderabad.
  - It offers online tracking, escalation, a call centre "to help illiterate grievants" and a mobile app.
- MEA's telephone-enquiry page lists "Madad Helpline 1800-11-3090, https://www.madad.gov.in". — [MEA Telephonic Enquiries](https://www.mea.gov.in/contact-tele-inquiry)
- OWRC helplines: **1800-11-3090** (24x7 toll-free) and **0124-2341002** (toll number). The PBSK line is listed on the same page as 8004-6342. — [MEA Consular Complaints & Grievances](https://www.mea.gov.in/consular-complaints-and-grievances.htm), updated 29 Jun 2026
- The OWRC is a 24x7x365 helpdesk in 11 languages, including **Telugu**. — [MEA OWRC & MRC note](https://www.mea.gov.in/images/pdf/owrc-and-mrc.pdf)
  - Numbers: toll-free 1800-11-3090 in India; +91-124-2341002 for emigrants overseas.
  - It is linked to Migrant Resource Centres (MRCs), including Hyderabad. MRC walk-in hours are 10:00 to 17:00, Monday to Friday.
- How to file on MADAD. These steps come from mission guidance, as summarised in search results, and are not fully verified because madad.gov.in itself was unreachable. — [MEA FAQ on registering complaints](https://mea.gov.in/faq-registering-complaint.htm); [Embassy Abu Dhabi guidelines PDF](https://www.indembassyuae.gov.in/pdf/Guidelines%20for%20applicants%20for%20registering%20grievance.pdf)
  1. Register as a new user.
  2. Activate the account through the email link, or an OTP if you have an Indian mobile.
  3. Log in and choose "Register Grievances".
  4. Track the case with the grievance ID.
  - Visa, passport and attestation issues are out of scope for MADAD.
- eMigrate was rolled out in all PoE offices from 25 Sep 2014. — [MEA Consular Complaints & Grievances](https://www.mea.gov.in/consular-complaints-and-grievances.htm)
  - No separate eMigrate-branded helpline was verifiable, because the emigrate.gov.in site is JavaScript-only.
  - MEA lists 1800-11-3090 as the OWRC/Madad line.

### Inferences
- A voice agent should give workers inside India, or their families, 1800-11-3090. It is free, runs 24x7, and Telugu is available.
- Workers abroad should use the mission line first, then +91-124-2341002.
- Point to madad.gov.in for written grievances about unpaid wages, detention or repatriation.

## Q3. Telangana government support: TOMCOM, NRI cell, ex gratia, Gulf/NRI policy, Prajavani

### Takeaway
- Telangana pays **Rs 5 lakh ex gratia** to families of workers who died in seven Gulf countries on or after **7 Dec 2023**. This is under GO Ms 205 and GO 216, from Sep–Oct 2024. The district collector sanctions the payment.
- A "Pravasi Prajavani" grievance counter opened at Praja Bhavan, Hyderabad on 27 Sep 2024.
- An NRI Advisory Committee was formed in April 2025 to draft an NRI/Gulf policy and a welfare board. No final policy was found.
- Official TOMCOM contacts could not be verified because the site was unreachable.

### Cited Findings
- Ex gratia: Rs 5 lakh for families of migrant workers who die in Iraq, Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia or the UAE, "without considering the cause of death". — [Siasat, 9 Oct 2024](https://www.siasat.com/telangana-govt-issues-norms-on-ex-gratia-to-kin-of-deceased-gulf-workers-3109981)
  - Eligibility: deaths on or after 7 Dec 2023.
  - Documents: death certificate, cancelled passport, work visa or employment contract, and bank details.
  - The district collector verifies the documents and sanctions payment by direct bank transfer.
  - Status: unverified (news). The GO text itself was not retrieved.
- GO 205 (16 Sep 2024) set up the scheme, and GO 216 (16 Oct 2024) issued the guidelines. — [Telangana Today, 27 Nov 2024](https://telanganatoday.com/gulf-ex-gratia-denied-to-migrant-workers-from-telangana-who-die-in-other-countries)
  - This conflicts with [Siasat](https://www.siasat.com/telangana-govt-issues-norms-on-ex-gratia-to-kin-of-deceased-gulf-workers-3109981), which says the guidelines were issued "Monday, October 7".
  - Telangana Today lists "Iran" where Siasat lists Iraq, which is probably an error.
  - Families and the Gulf JAC want the scheme extended to other ECR countries and to deaths before the cutoff.
- One search summary said the cutoff was 12 Dec 2023. Most sources say **7 Dec 2023**. — [search results incl. Deccan Chronicle](https://deccanchronicle.com/southern-states/telangana/telangana-to-give-rs-5-lakh-ex-gratia-to-gulf-victims-1824162)
- Payments made: reportedly 169 families were paid Rs 5 lakh each in 2024–2025, a total of Rs 8.45 crore. Delays were reported in Jagtial and Karimnagar. — search summary citing [Siasat](https://www.siasat.com/telangana-govt-sanctions-rs-1-55-crore-ex-gratia-for-families-of-deceased-gulf-workers-3307190) and [Deccan Chronicle](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/southern-states/telangana/jagtial-migrant-workers-kin-still-await-ex-gratia-1934581). Status: unverified (news).

### Inferences
- The voice agent can tell a family member who reports a Gulf death that they may claim Rs 5 lakh through the district collector, if the death happened abroad in one of the seven listed countries on or after 7 Dec 2023. The required documents are listed above.
- Telangana has no confirmed statewide Gulf-worker toll-free helpline. The CM promised one, but none was found. The voice agent should use MEA and mission lines for emergencies.

- No official contact page was found for the Telangana NRI department (GAD-NRI) or the Telangana Bhavan, Delhi control room.
- There is no evidence on whether a Gulf Workers Welfare Board or NRI policy was notified in 2025–2026.
