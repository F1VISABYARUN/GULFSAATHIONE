The data behind the answers.

Before Gulf Saathi says a figure or a number, it relies on something read from an official source. Here is which data we use, why we need it, how current it is, and where it falls short.

Figures here were read on official pages on the dates shown and can change. Always confirm with the embassy or emigrate.gov.in. Gulf Saathi never states a detail it has not read from an official source.

What is kept about you

  • A case holds your phone number, your name if you gave it, what you told us, caseworkers’ notes and call outcomes. A safety phrase only if you chose one; a family phone number only with consent.
  • The message to a family contains only the case number.
  • Gulf Saathi’s call server does not save call audio. Speech, translation and phone providers process what you say to understand and answer you, and have their own rules.
  • The number you type in the “Get a call” form is used to place the call; we keep only a one-way fingerprint of it to enforce a daily limit.
  • Case files are available only to authorised caseworkers.
