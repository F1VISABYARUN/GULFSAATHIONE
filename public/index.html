THE WORKER’S JOURNEY

Before departure. On arrival. At work.

01

Before money changes hands

Say the agent’s name, licence number, fee and visa type. Saathi checks the available register and flags details to verify before paying.

02

Keep the promise on record

Record the employer, job, salary, hours and fee. When an amount is unclear, Saathi asks you to say it again.

03

Compare what happens next

Who picked you up? Do you have your passport? Were you paid? A caseworker can review differences between the promise and the worker’s account.

ON THE LINE

Start with one question.

Choose an example to see how Saathi can help.

“The agent said it’s a visit visa.”

Describe the job and the visa offered. Saathi flags the mismatch and explains which documents to check before paying.

“They haven’t paid my salary.”

Say what you were promised and what you actually received. The difference can be recorded for a caseworker to review.

“I need a pharmacy nearby.”

Name the city and a building or street you can see. Saathi can search from that landmark and ask again if the name is unclear.

MORE THAN A TRANSLATOR

A risk-intelligence layer for every stage.

Gulf Saathi is not only a phone line that translates. It checks promises against official records, notices danger on a call, and keeps a case a person can act on.

BEFORE

Before you pay or fly

  • Check an agent against the government’s registered list.
  • Check a fee against the legal limit.
  • Spot a visit visa offered for work.
  • Record the promise, and get a briefing call before the flight.
DURING THE CALL

On the phone, in the moment

  • Hears Telugu and notices danger words such as a taken passport or unpaid salary.
  • Reads back amounts and numbers before they are saved.
  • Finds a pharmacy, hospital, police station or embassy near a landmark, with directions.
  • Explains what to do when a visa is ending, using only fines and fees read on official pages.
  • Speaks Arabic for you after you approve each message, and tells you in Telugu what they said. (Still being tested.)
AFTER THE CALL

The case, and what happens next

  • Saves a case file with the facts, the risk level and a timeline for a caseworker.
  • Phones to check after landing and then every week. Silence raises an alert.
  • Can send the family one message with the case number when the risk is high, only with consent.
  • Helps prepare the facts for a MADAD complaint. The worker or a caseworker files it on madad.gov.in; Saathi does not file it.

HOW IT STARTED

It started by listening to families.

  1. Listened

    Sat at Pravasi Prajavani and heard real cases from Gulf workers’ families: wrong jobs, unpaid wages, and months of waiting for papers.

  2. Built

    Weekly Telugu check-in calls, fee checks before departure, and ready case files for officials.

  3. Demoed

    Showed it to the Pravasi Prajavani team, Nov 2026.

  4. Next: pilot

    We were told we can start a pilot with Gulf worker families in Telangana, starting with Jagtial.

WHAT WE HEARD

What we heard at Pravasi Prajavani.

On 30 October 2026 we sat in on the Gulf workers’ grievance desk at Praja Bhavan, Hyderabad.

Today

  • No simple Telugu phone line the worker knows to call
  • Help starts only when the family finds out and travels to Hyderabad
  • Officials spend weeks collecting the employer, visa, agent and route before the embassy can act
  • Workers can’t check what the agent tells them

With Saathi

  • The agent’s promise is recorded in Telugu before the worker pays
  • The route, visa, employer, fees and document details are recorded from day one
  • Weekly check-in calls and a Telugu help line
  • The desk gets a ready case file

FOR GOVERNMENT & DISTRICT TEAMS

Connect a village phone call
to public support.

eMigrate publishes recruitment information. MADAD accepts consular grievances. A family hearing a promise by phone still has to capture the details, understand the terms abroad and bring both accounts to someone who can act.

Start in one district

A district team could enrol consenting workers before departure, share the phone line and explain which details are kept.

Assign a caseworker

A designated team could review flagged cases, contact workers when safe and refer them through the appropriate official channel.

Measure the outcome

Track time to first human response, incorrectly captured facts and cases resolved before expanding the pilot.
